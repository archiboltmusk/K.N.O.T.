## 💍 Overview

**K.N.O.T.** is a modern, high-intent relationship platform built specifically for working professionals (ages 25–35) seeking serious commitment and marriage. 

By bridging **cultural heritage & celestial calculations** with **real-world psychological and lifestyle compatibility**, K.N.O.T. provides an authentic, pressure-free alternative to both casual dating apps and traditional transactional matrimony sites.

---

## 🎯 The Problem We Solve

* **The Dating App Fatigue:** Platforms like Tinder and Bumble encourage endless swiping, low intent, ghosting, and superficial hookup culture.
* **The Traditional Matrimony Disconnect:** Legacy matrimonial portals often feel clinical, awkward, resume-driven, and heavily dictated by relatives without prioritizing emotional chemistry.
* **The Ethical Void in Astrology:** Most astrology platforms monetize through anxiety, fear-mongering, and dubious claims. K.N.O.T. uses celestial calculations purely as a reflective, cultural compass combined with practical compatibility.

## ✨ Key Features (MVP)

1. **High-Intent Verification:**
   * Identity & profession verification (LinkedIn / work email) to ensure zero spam and verified intent.
2. **Curated Daily Drops:**
   * 3 to 5 curated introductions delivered each morning. No gamified, addictive infinite swiping.
3. **Dual Compatibility Card:**
   * Transparent breakdown showing both cultural alignment and lifestyle compatibility side-by-side.
4. **Pre-Marital Conversation Prompts:**
   * Structured icebreakers on the big life topics couples often avoid: finances, children, in-laws, and life goals.
5. **Private Family Sharing:**
   * Keep parents involved with an opt-in "Family Circle" mode where users can share selected profiles on their own terms.

## 🛡️ Our Truthful Standard

* **Zero Fear-Mongering:** We never send panic-inducing notifications, warn of "curses," or charge money to "remedy" chart alignments.
* **Transparent Calculations:** Astrological data is generated using standard ephemeris positioning (NASA JPL / Swiss Ephemeris data) and presented with constructive, life-affirming context.
* **Data Privacy First:** Encrypted personal data, birth times, and communications.

## 🛠️ Tech Stack Roadmap

* **Mobile App:** React Native / Flutter (iOS & Android)
* **Backend:** Node.js / Python (FastAPI)
* **Celestial Engine:** Swiss Ephemeris (`pyswisseph` / `swisseph`) for planetary math
* **Database:** PostgreSQL with Row-Level Security
* **Authentication:** Supabase Auth / Clerk with OAuth & Work Verification
