## 2. The 3 Core Strategic Directives

### Directive 1: Position as an "Intentional Marriage Platform", NOT an "Astrology App"
* Astrology is a **cultural comfort layer and conversation catalyst**, not the whole brand identity.
* Positioning as "Dating for Marriage" attracts high-intent, serious professionals who are tired of swipe culture.

### Directive 2: Solve the "Cold-Start" Liquidity Trap via Progressive Disclosure
Forcing users to enter exact birth times and coordinates during onboarding causes an **80%+ drop-off rate**. K.N.O.T. breaks onboarding into two clean phases:

```
[ Phase 1: Rapid Onboarding (< 90 seconds) ]
  • Work verification (LinkedIn / Corporate Email)
  • Birth date (Day/Month/Year for Sun & General Moon sign)
  • 4 Core Values: Finances, Career Pace, Family Dynamics, Communication Style
  • 3 Profile Photos + Intent Statement
            │
            ▼
[ Phase 2: Progressive Unlock (Post-Match) ]
  • Mutual match occurs
  • Users opt-in to enter exact birth times & cities
  • Unlocks the full Dual-Layer Compatibility Card & Pre-Marital Conversation Deck
```

### Directive 3: High-Margin, Truthful Monetization (Zero Panic Calls)
Instead of predatory per-minute calls answering *"Is my ex coming back?"*:

1. **High-Intent Membership Tier (₹1,500 – ₹2,500 / month):**
   * High willingness-to-pay because serious users want to filter out casual daters, time-wasters, and scammers.
2. **The Pre-Marital Synergy Dossier (₹999 one-time):**
   * An editorial-grade, beautifully typeset digital report analyzing practical values, financial habits, and celestial alignment for couples approaching engagement.
3. **Vetted Pre-Marital Coaching Marketplace:**
   * Commission on sessions with certified, progressive relationship counselors for structured discussions on finances and living arrangements.

## 3. Competitive Moats
1. **Verified Liquidity:** Verified employment/education creates immediate trust among top-tier urban professionals.
2. **Ethical Standard:** 100% transparent astronomical calculations. Never charging for "curses" or "remedies."
3. **Family Inclusivity Without Loss of Agency:** The "Family Circle" link lets couples share approved profiles with parents respectfully, preserving modern independence.
